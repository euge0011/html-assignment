About Me

My name is Jackley Eugene. It is current my 1st year as a student at Algonquin College, where I am studying Interactive Media Design. I was raised in Haiti, lived in Florida, New Jersey, France and now I am currently living in Ottawa, Canada. I am 22 years old and I speak four languages. My family is a mix of Haitian, French, Cuban and Arab which has given me a unique perspective on life, culture and identity. I have always been interested in technology and photography, which is what led me to pursue a career in the field of Interactive Media Design. I moved to Canada a few months ago to kind of get a reset from living in Miami, it is much calmer here and I am enjoying the change of scenery, interests and surroundings that came with moving to a new country.


Outside of school, I enjoy spending quality time with my family and friends even though most of my family is in Montreal. Canada has made me fall in love with nature because of the beautiful scenery and the few outdoor activities available which has made me enjoy sightseeing, running and walking downtown Sometimes I do miss being able to go out every weekend to party or go on trips from my time living in Miami, but Canada has given me a new perspective on enjoyment ... enjoying the little things in life as much as the big things because you never know when you will have the opportunity to experience them again. I also enjoy trying out different cuisines, going hiking, watching movies, and just spending time with myself which I didn't get the chance to do back then because of my schedule and the many distractions that came with living in a big city. Overall, I am grateful for the opportunities and experiences that have helped me progressively become the person I am today, and I look forward to what the future holds.
